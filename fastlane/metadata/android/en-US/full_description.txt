Radioold is a musical time machine: pick a country on an antique world
map, pick a decade from the 1900s to the 2010s, pick a mood, and hear
real music of that place and time.

* Antique world map - 180 countries, pinch-zoom and drag-pan
* Decades 1900s through 2010s, plus "Now"
* Moods: Any / Slow / Fast / Weird
* Background playback with media notification controls
* Taxi mode: multi-stop journeys across countries and decades
* Liner notes per track: album, label, songwriter, year, favourites

Radioold is a clean-room client for the radiooooo.com station catalog
(hence the NonFreeNet anti-feature: the music stream comes from that
proprietary service). No code from radiooooo.com is used. Music and
cover art belong to their owners.
